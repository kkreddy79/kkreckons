# Welcome email series (draft for approval)

Three emails sent by a beehiiv automation to every new subscriber, triggered on sign-up.
Nothing is live until Krishnakanth approves the copy; then it is set up in beehiiv
(Automations → New → trigger "Subscription created") with these three emails.

Links use the site's real pages. `{{rp_refer_url}}` is beehiiv's merge tag for the reader's personal referral link
(only used once the referral program is on; until then email 3 uses the plain share line in brackets).

---

## Email 1 — sent straight away

**Subject:** Welcome to KKReckons: start with this
**Preview text:** One visual briefing a day. Here's how to get the most from it.

Hi there,

Thanks for subscribing. Every morning you'll get one short email: the day's most important stories on AI, markets,
business, India and the world, each with a graphic, the key points, why it matters and the other side.

Three ways to use it:

- **Short on time?** Read "Today in 30 seconds" at the top of each edition.
- **Want the full picture?** Every story links to the original source. A small lock means the source may need a subscription;
  where there's a free version, it's linked too.
- **Missed a few days?** "The week in 8 stories" picks the best of the week: https://www.kkreckons.com/week

Start with today's edition: https://www.kkreckons.com

Krishnakanth

## Email 2 — sent 2 days later

**Subject:** The one number I check every day
**Preview text:** What the AI Stress Index tells you, in two minutes.

Hi,

Most AI news is about what the technology can do. The money behind it gets less attention, and that's where trouble usually
shows up first.

So I built the **AI Stress Index**: one 0–100 reading of strain in AI stocks, AI financing and credit, updated after every
US market close.

- **0–24 Normal**, **25–49 Elevated**, **50–74 Severe**, **75–100 Critical**.
- When a story in the briefing is about AI debt or credit stress, it's tagged "Index", so you can see what's moving it.
- The full dashboard shows each gauge and how it has moved: https://www.kkreckons.com/ai-stress-index

It's a gauge, not a forecast, and not investment advice. But if it moves into a new band, you'll see it at the top of that
day's edition.

Krishnakanth
