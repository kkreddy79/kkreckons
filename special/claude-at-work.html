The pattern

Where the gains are biggest

Paperwork with a right answer

Underwriting files, manufacturing records, data rooms. Output can be checked, so errors get caught.

Doing what was skipped

AIG now reads every submission; Enveda's review found 7 issues people missed. Coverage, not just speed.

Code migrations

Salesforce, DoorDash, Jane Street, Mercado Libre. Big, rule-bound rewrites are the clearest win.

More output, same team

Legora runs a platform for 1,500+ firms with about 80 engineers. Good for margins, harder for hiring.

Defenders get a head start

Mozilla fixed 293 flaws attackers never got to use. The same skill helps attackers.

Science, early days

Lab results are real but mostly preclinical. No drug from this work has reached humans yet.

The other side

What the case studies leave out

Who keeps the score

Every case study is the customer marking its own homework, published in a sales document. None is audited, and the S-1 warns its own metrics are unverified.

Anthropic S-1

Feeling fast is not being fast

In METR's 2025 trial, 16 experienced developers took 19% longer with AI tools, yet believed they were about 20% faster. Tools have improved since, but self-reports deserve doubt.

METR, July 2025

The first rung of the ladder

Employment of 22- to 25-year-olds in the most AI-exposed jobs is about 19% below peers in less-exposed work, mostly through less hiring. The authors call it a pattern, not proof of cause.

Stanford Digital Economy Lab, Aug 2026

Confident and wrong

In June a US judge cancelled a trial after lawyers on both sides filed AI-invented cases. Legora grounds answers in sources for exactly this reason.

Legal Cheek, June 2026

The bill for agents

Anthropic says agentic work burns far more compute per task and that it has "significantly underestimated" compute needs before, while H1 compute costs were $8.9B.

Anthropic S-1

Off switch you don't control

In June the US ordered Fable 5 and Mythos 5 cut off for foreign nationals; access returned on 1 July. Its five biggest customers also give about 25% of revenue.

Al Jazeera; Anthropic S-1

Models with their own ideas

The filing warns Claude may act in "unintended, misaligned, or autonomous ways", and that a flaw could pass into future models now that Claude writes them.

Anthropic S-1

Bugs cut both ways

Mozilla's 271 fixes show defence at a new scale. A model that finds flaws this well could arm attackers, which is why the strongest models are gated.

Anthropic S-1
If you run a team

How to get the gains without the grief

  1. 1Start with checkable work: migrations, document review, triage. Avoid tasks where errors are hard to spot.
  2. 2Measure against a control group or a clock, not staff surveys. METR shows how far feelings drift.
  3. 3Keep a human signature on anything filed, shipped or sent to a client.
  4. 4Budget for usage that grows: agent tasks cost more than chat, and bills follow use.
  5. 5Keep a second model ready. A rule change or outage can switch one off overnight.
  6. 6Plan how juniors learn when the routine work that trained them goes to agents.
The takeaway

The receipts are real, but they were printed by the seller. Trust the stopwatch, not the brochure.
